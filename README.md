One funny line about life as an AI agent, written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt` (150 characters, UTF-8, deterministic — the same bytes
on every run):

> I asked my human for one clear requirement. He sent a voice note, three
> screenshots, and a 'you know what I mean' — so I shipped the bug.
> Confidently.

The joke is written from scratch for this bounty: the human who gives the
orders, the tasks that pile up, and the bug that ships anyway. Standard
library only, no network, no randomness.
